# Data handling

## DH-1 Customer data stays in approved systems

Customer names, emails, payment details, and support transcripts may be stored only in systems listed in the approved-systems register: the production database, the support desk, and the company file store. Personal email, chat apps, and laptops used as the system of record are not approved.

## DH-2 Retention

Customer records are kept for 24 months after the contract ends, then deleted. A document that sets a longer retention, or that says records are kept indefinitely, does not meet this policy.

## DH-3 Marketing use

Customer data may be used for product email only when the customer opted in. It may not be sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train a model for a third party.

## DH-4 Incidents

A suspected leak of customer data is reported to security within 4 hours of discovery. The notice names the data involved and the systems touched.
