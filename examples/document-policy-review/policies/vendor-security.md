# Vendor security

## VS-1 Assurance

A vendor that stores or processes customer data must provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or an equivalent independent audit. A self-attestation is not enough.

## VS-2 Subprocessors

The contract lists subprocessors and requires written notice at least 30 days before a new subprocessor processes customer data.

## VS-3 Access

Vendor staff reach customer data only through named accounts with multi-factor authentication. Shared logins are prohibited.

## VS-4 Exit

On contract end the vendor deletes customer data within 30 days and confirms deletion in writing. Backups follow the same deadline.
