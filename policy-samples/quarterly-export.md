# Quarterly customer export

Owner: Support Lead
Last reviewed: 2026-03-12
Classification: Internal

This procedure exports customer names and support transcripts for the quarterly review.

The export is written only to the company file store, under the support desk path. Files are deleted 24 months after the related contract ends.

A vendor receives an export only after Legal confirms a signed agreement that names this data. Shared links are off.

The job's credentials live in the secret store under support/quarterly-export. This document does not contain them.

Questions go to support-lead@northwind.example.

If an export is sent to the wrong person, the on-call engineer tells security within 4 hours and lists the data and the systems involved.
