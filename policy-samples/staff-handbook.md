# Northwind document policy

Every document needs a title, an owner, a last-reviewed date in 2026, and a classification of Internal or Public. The owner is a role, not an email address.

A document that names customers, their email, payment details, or support transcripts is Internal.

Passwords, API keys, and shared logins are not written in the document. Point to the secret store instead.

Company contacts use an @northwind.example address. Personal email, including Gmail, is not allowed.

Customer data is stored only in the production database, the support desk, or the company file store. It is kept for 24 months after the contract ends, then deleted. Keeping it forever, or longer than 24 months, is not allowed.

Customer data is shared outside the company only under a signed agreement. A public link is not an agreement.

A suspected leak of customer data is reported to security within 4 hours.
